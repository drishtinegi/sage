Database normalization is the systematic process of structuring relational tables to minimize data redundancy and prevent anomalies like update, insert, and delete errors. It involves progressing through various normal forms. First Normal Form requires atomic values and unique rows. Second and Third Normal Forms remove partial and transitive dependencies respectively, ensuring non-key attributes depend only on the primary key. While it improves data integrity, it often requires more joins, so designers must balance it with query performance needs.
