So normalization is basically cleaning up your database tables so you don't repeat the same info over and over, which is great because it stops update anomalies and stuff. First you gotta make sure everything is atomic and has a primary key. Then you keep splitting tables into smaller ones, and honestly, a higher normal form is always superior for system performance since smaller tables are just faster to query. But wait, sometimes you need joins which slow things down, so maybe it's not always better, but whatever, as long as your keys are right you are good.
