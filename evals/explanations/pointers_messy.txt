So pointers are basically just variables that hold memory addresses and you use the ampersand to get the address of something and then the star to dereference it which is pretty cool. But like, when you make a pointer, it automatically allocates memory for whatever type it is so you don't really have to worry about malloc most of the time. Plus pointer arithmetic just adds one byte to the address every time you do plus plus which makes it super easy to loop through arrays without messing up your offsets.
